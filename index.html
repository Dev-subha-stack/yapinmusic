How it works

Guess the Song

  • We show you a band and give you lives: 8 on Easy, 6 on Medium, or 4 on Hard.
  • Type a song title and choose it from the suggestions.
  • Find any 3 of the band’s hit songs to win. A wrong answer uses 1 life and reveals a clue.
  • Hints are free on Easy, cost 1 life on Medium, and are turned off on Hard.

Guess the Band

  • We show you a song (and its release year, except on Hard).
  • Guess the band. You get 9 tries on Easy, 7 on Medium, or 5 on Hard.
  • After a wrong guess, compare the clues for country, genre, formation year, and first letter.
  • Hints are free on Easy, cost 1 guess on Medium, and are turned off on Hard.

Difficulty

  • Easy: famous bands and songs, with more lives or guesses.
  • Medium: more bands and songs, with fewer tries.
  • Hard: less familiar songs, fewer tries, and less help.
